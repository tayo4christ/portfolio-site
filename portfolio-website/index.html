About Me

My introduction

Omotayo Emmanuel Omoyemi is an AI researcher, educational technologist and computer scientist whose work focuses on artificial intelligence, accessibility and inclusive technology. His work spans advanced research in gesture recognition and assistive technologies, including the development of an AI-driven Makaton-to-English translation system presented at the ICSIE 2024 conference and published in the ACM Digital Library. He has also worked on AI approaches for speech accessibility and multimodal systems designed to support people with communication difficulties. Omotayo is a Fellow Member of the British Computer Society (BCS), a Senior member of IEEE, and a Fellow of Institute of Stem Innovation and Excellence (FISIE).
